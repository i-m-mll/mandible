# Mandible design and performance audit

## Objective

Make ordinary Mandible operations easier to reason about, more responsive, and
reliable by correcting shared ownership and eliminating redundant machinery.
Select work from current behavior and code; historical issue state is supporting
evidence rather than a requirement to preserve obsolete designs.
